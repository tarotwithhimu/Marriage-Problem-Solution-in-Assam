What people bring to a session

Common marriage problems in Assam

Every relationship is different — a consultation should start by understanding your actual problem, not assume every marriage issue has the same root.

Communication problems

Small conversations turning into arguments, feelings left unspoken, difficult topics postponed again and again.

Frequent arguments

Repeated conflict over money, household work, parenting, career or how time together is spent.

Emotional distance

"We are together, but we don't feel connected anymore." Less conversation, less affection, quiet isolation.

Family interference

Tension involving parents, in-laws, living arrangements or differing family expectations.

Financial stress

Disagreements over spending, savings, loans, business investments or shared household expenses.

Delayed marriage

Uncertainty about timing, compatibility concerns before marriage, or repeated proposals not working out.

Compatibility concerns

Personality differences, communication styles and mismatched expectations in love or arranged marriages.

Separation & reconciliation

Wondering whether reconciliation is possible, whether to keep waiting, or how to move forward after a breakup.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What is the best marriage problem solution in Assam?

There is no single solution for every marriage. The right approach depends on the actual issue — communication, financial stress, family conflict, emotional distance or compatibility all call for different guidance.

Can astrology solve husband-wife problems?

Astrology can offer a traditional framework for reflection, but it cannot guarantee that a problem will disappear. Communication and practical relationship work remain essential alongside any consultation.

Can an astrologer help with marriage problems in Assam?

Yes. A personalised consultation can discuss marriage and relationship concerns using birth-chart analysis and relevant astrological factors such as the seventh house, Venus, Jupiter and planetary periods.

Can I consult a marriage astrologer online?

Yes. Online consultations over WhatsApp or call make this accessible from anywhere in Assam, including Jorhat, Dibrugarh, Nagaon, Tezpur, Silchar and beyond.

How much does a marriage astrology consultation cost?

The Basic Astrology Consultation is ₹1,199 for around 20–25 minutes. The Detailed Astrology Consultation is ₹2,499 for a broader 45-minute horoscope analysis.

Can astrology predict divorce or whether my spouse will return?

Astrology can be used to discuss relationship patterns and timing indicators, but no responsible reading can guarantee another person's decision. Reconciliation depends on mutual willingness.

What birth details are required for a consultation?

Generally your date of birth, most accurate time of birth, and place of birth. For compatibility readings, both partners' birth details are needed. If your birth time is uncertain, mention this before the session.
